Minesweeper

🚩 10 ⏱ 0 🏆 —

Click any cell to start

  • Left click — reveal a cell
  • Right click — place or remove a flag
  • Middle click — chord (see below)

Chording opens all remaining neighbours of a revealed number in one go, once you have flagged exactly that many cells around it. If one of those flags is wrong the chord uncovers a mine and ends the game.
